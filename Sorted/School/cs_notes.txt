COURSE: CS-421 – Natural Language Processing
Lecture 8: Large Language Models and Transformers

Key concepts:
- Self-attention mechanism (Queries, Keys, Values)
- Multi-head attention
- Positional encoding
- Pretraining and fine-tuning

Example of attention calculation:
  Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QK^T / sqrt(d_k)) V

The Transformer architecture consists of:
  - Encoder stack (N=6 layers)
  - Decoder stack (N=6 layers)
  - Feed-forward networks
  - Residual connections and layer norm

Applications:
  - Text generation (GPT)
  - Translation (BERT)
  - Summarisation (T5)

References:
  Vaswani et al. "Attention is All You Need" (2017)
  Brown et al. "Language Models are Few-Shot Learners" (2020)
